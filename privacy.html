Privacy

Privacy information

Last updated: September 2026

Football Buying Network (“FBN”) is currently being developed and tested by Lee Holness in the United Kingdom. This page explains how information is handled during the validation pilot and when you use this website.

Website data

This launch website does not currently use advertising trackers, analytics cookies, user accounts or an online contact form. Standard technical logs may be processed by the website hosting provider as part of operating and securing the service.

Information provided by clubs

If a club chooses to participate in the FBN pilot, information may be provided by email, telephone or other agreed methods. This may include contact details, purchasing categories, approximate spend, supplier information, renewal timing and upcoming purchasing needs.

FBN does not ask clubs to provide bank details, card details, passwords, payroll records or other information that is not necessary for the pilot.

How information may be used

  • to communicate with clubs about the FBN pilot;
  • to understand purchasing needs and identify potential buying opportunities;
  • to compare compatible demand across participating clubs;
  • to approach potential suppliers about relevant aggregated demand; and
  • to evaluate whether the FBN model produces useful savings or administrative benefits.

Sharing information

FBN will not sell a club’s individual information. Where supplier conversations are needed, only information reasonably necessary to test or quote an opportunity should be shared. Aggregated or anonymised information may be used to understand wider purchasing patterns.

Data retention and security

Information should only be retained for as long as it is reasonably needed for the pilot, follow-up, record keeping or applicable legal obligations. Reasonable steps will be taken to keep working records secure and limit unnecessary access.

Your choices

Participation in the pilot is voluntary. A club can decline an opportunity, stop participating or ask questions about information it has provided.

Contact

For privacy questions relating to the current FBN pilot, contact Lee Holness at lee87mh@gmail.com.
